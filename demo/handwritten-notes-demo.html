1 Polity — Vice President & Union Executive ⚖️
Q1 Which branch of government is the Vice President of India a part of?
A Legislative only
B Executive only
C Judicial
D Both Executive and Legislative ✔
✅ Answer: D — The VP is part of the Union Executive (Part V, Chapter I: President, VP, PM, Council of Ministers, Attorney General) and the Legislature, since he is the ex-officio Chairperson of the Rajya Sabha.
🔑 Key Facts

Vice President — Key Articles

  • Articles 63–71 — Vice President provisions
  • Article 72 — Pardoning power of the President
  • Article 71 — Supreme Court has exclusive jurisdiction over disputes regarding the election of President / VP (similar to Art 131's original jurisdiction)

2 Biology — Vacuoles & Cell Organelles 🧫
Q2 Which statement is NOT correct regarding vacuoles?
A "Vacuoles are generally larger in animal cells than in plant cells" — this is FALSE ✔
✅ Answer: FALSE — vacuoles are actually LARGER in plant cells, occupying 50–90% of cell volume (single large vacuole, permanent). Animal cells have multiple SMALLER, temporary vacuoles.

🧠 Memory Trick
🌱 Meristematic tissue LACKS vacuoles — but has a prominent nucleus and dense cytoplasm. This is a frequently repeated fact!

3 Polity — Constitutional Schedules 📋
Q3 Which of the following is correctly matched with respect to the Schedules of the Constitution?
A Second Schedule — Anti-defection law
B Ninth Schedule — Official languages
C Tenth Schedule — Anti-defection provisions ✔
D Eighth Schedule — Land reform laws
✅ Answer: C — The Tenth Schedule deals with anti-defection provisions (added by the 52nd Amendment, 1985).
🔑 Key Facts

High-yield Schedules

  • 2nd — Salaries & allowances of officials
  • 8th — 22 official languages
  • 9th — Land reform laws (protected from judicial review, added by 1st Amendment)
  • 10th — Anti-defection law
