Why I built PrepPath

I was preparing for SSC and facing the same problems every aspirant knows.

Plans were spread across notebooks, lectures were hard to track, and mock-test mistakes rarely became a clear next action. The hardest part was not finding more content—it was staying organised and consistent.

That struggle became the idea for PrepPath: one honest, practical workspace built around how aspirants actually study. I am building the tool I needed myself, so every student can spend less energy managing preparation and more energy making progress.
